Retrieval Evaluation Results
============================

Retrieval corpus
----------------
Historical AmazonHelp customer-response cases: 116,979

Embedding model:
BAAI/bge-base-en-v1.5

Similarity:
Cosine similarity between normalized BGE embeddings.

Evaluation note
---------------
The historical cases do not have human intent labels.

Therefore, retrieval was evaluated using intent consistency:
a retrieved historical case is considered a hit when its
classifier-derived intent matches the manually labelled intent
of the held-out test query.

This measures whether retrieval finds cases with similar
intent. It is NOT a direct measure of human-rated semantic
relevance.

Cross-Encoder Reranking Experiment
----------------------------------

Reranker:
cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2

Hit@1:  60.00%
Hit@3:  78.75%
Hit@5:  83.75%
Hit@10: 90.00%


Decision
--------
The cross-encoder reranker was not used in the final pipeline.

It reduced Hit@1 from 65.00% to 60.00% and produced no
improvement at Hit@10. The additional inference complexity
was therefore not justified by the observed improvement.
