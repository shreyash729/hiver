AmazonHelp Support Agent
Response Evaluation Results
===========================

Evaluation setup
----------------
Held-out examples evaluated: 10

The examples were drawn from the manually labelled test set.

Each response was evaluated by:
1. LLM-as-judge
2. Independent human rating


LLM-as-Judge Rubric
-------------------
Each criterion is scored from 1 to 5.

resolution_relevance
    Does the response address the customer's actual problem?

grounding
    Are the claims and guidance supported by the historical
    support evidence?

helpfulness
    Does the response provide a useful and appropriate next step?

unsupported_claims
    Does the response avoid unsupported claims, invented policies,
    guarantees, or pretending to access the customer's account/order?

overall
    Overall quality of the support response.


LLM Judge Results
-----------------
Average resolution_relevance: 4.0 / 5
Average grounding:            4.7 / 5
Average helpfulness:          3.8 / 5
Average unsupported_claims:   5.0 / 5
Average overall:              4.0 / 5


Human vs LLM Judge Agreement
----------------------------
Number of examples: 10

LLM overall scores:
4, 4, 3, 4, 5, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5

Human overall scores:
4, 4, 3, 4, 5, 3, 4, 4, 4, 4

Exact agreement: 90.0%

Spearman correlation: 0.8845
p-value: 0.0007


Interpretation
--------------
The LLM judge showed high agreement with the independent human
ratings on this small 10-example sample.

This should be treated as initial validation rather than strong
evidence of judge reliability because the sample size is small.

One disagreement occurred on a technical-support response where
the generated response stated that Amazon was aware of temporary
technical problems. The human evaluator considered this claim
insufficiently supported and scored it lower than the LLM judge.
